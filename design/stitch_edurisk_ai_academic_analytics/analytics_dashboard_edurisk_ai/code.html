Data Standard & Privacy

Student records anonymized via Portuguese school administration identifiers. All 33 demographic, familial, and scholastic attributes preserved intact without synthetic imputation.

Curriculum Horizon

Targets end-of-year Mathematics exam (G3). Period 1 (G1) and Period 2 (G2) formative evaluations serve as intermediate benchmark checkpoints throughout the academic calendar.

Institutional Deployment

Currently configured for early-warning flagging at Week 6 of the academic term. Model predictions trigger academic tutor notifications when probability score exceeds 0.45.
